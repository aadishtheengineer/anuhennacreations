Our Passion & Vision

Hi, my name is Anu Nakrani. I've always had a passion for art since I was a little kid, and now I'm using it for my job. I don't do this for the money—I do it because I love it.

I'm channeling my art skills and my art passion into something meaningful, and I truly believe that picking the henna road was the correct choice for me. Every design I create is thoughtfully crafted to reflect your unique personality and the significance of your event.

We invite you to experience the therapeutic nature of natural, clean henna paste preparation and adornment on your most monumental day.
