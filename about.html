Meet Anu Nakrani

Welcome to Anu Henna Creations. Based proudly out of Morris Plains, New Jersey, my journey into the fine art of freehand mehndi stems from a deep appreciation for geometric symmetry, rhythmic balance, and cultural heritage storytelling.

What sets our studio apart is a steadfast refusal to rush the creative process. Every bridal drape and intricate pattern layout is strictly curated to mirror the individuality of the muse. From bold Rajasthani elements to delicate Arabic laces, we bring vision to life with precision.

We invite you to experience the therapeutic nature of natural, clean henna paste preparation and adornment on your most monumental day.
