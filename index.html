About Anu

Hi, my name is Anu Nakrani. I've always had a passion for art since I was a little kid, and now I'm using it for my job. I don't do this for the money—I do it because I love it. I'm channeling my art skills and my art passion into something meaningful, and I truly believe that picking the henna road was the correct choice for me.

Every design I create is thoughtfully crafted to reflect your unique personality and the significance of your event. I work exclusively with organic, natural henna paste that is safe, nourishing, and produces a rich, lasting stain. Your comfort and satisfaction are my top priorities, and I strive to make the henna application experience relaxing, memorable, and truly special.

Whether you're planning a wedding, engagement, festival celebration, or any special moment, I am here to bring your henna vision to life with artistry, care, and dedication. Henna is not just art for me—it's a celebration of culture, beauty, and personal expression.
